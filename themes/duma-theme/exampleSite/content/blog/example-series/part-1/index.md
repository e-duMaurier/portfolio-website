This is Part 1 of an example **series**. Parts are ordinary blog pages placed
inside the series directory; they are ordered by their `date` (oldest first) on
the series page.

The box at the bottom of this page is the series navigation
(`layouts/_partials/series-nav.html`), which links to the previous and next
parts and shows "Part N of M". It only appears when the page's parent section
sets `layout = 'series'`.
